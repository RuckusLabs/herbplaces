From finding your place to making it yours.

  1. Find your place

    Search the directory. If your place is missing, send a free listing request and follow its review status in Your Listing Requests.

  2. Claim your listing

    Once the listing is available, open it and choose Claim This Place. Sign in or create a free account, confirm your email, and share your connection to the business.

  3. Get approved

    Our team reviews your claim. Adding a listing and approving its managers are separate steps. You can follow your claim in My Places; no payment is taken during review.

  4. Make it your own

    After ownership approval, your billing contact can choose Bloom when checkout is enabled. All approved managers can then edit the place page, while the billing contact manages the subscription in My Places.

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I subscribe to Thrive today?

Thrive is not available for purchase. You can ask to join the invitation-only pilot. We agree the pilot scope and end date before access starts; an inquiry creates no payment or commitment.

How much does Thrive cost?

Thrive is $49 USD per month per place. We’ll confirm the included features and terms before subscriptions open.

Will customers buy products on Little Herb Places?

Visitors follow your links to shop, book, or register on your existing website. Little Herb Places does not take customer payments or manage your appointment calendar.

Does Thrive include promotion?

The pilot includes discovery through offering and event pages. Guaranteed newsletter placements and sponsored spotlights are not included. We will define inventory and reporting before selling those separately.

Should I wait for Thrive to claim my place?

You can request a listing or submit a free ownership claim now. Bloom is the option for maintaining a richer listing when checkout is enabled. You do not need to wait for Thrive.
