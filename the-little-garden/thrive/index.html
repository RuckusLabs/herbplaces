From finding your place to making it yours.

  1. Find your place

    Search the directory. If your place is missing, send a free listing request and follow its review status in Your Listing Requests.

  2. Claim your listing

    Once the listing is available, open it and choose Claim This Place. Sign in or create a free account, confirm your email, and share your connection to the business.

  3. Get approved

    Our team reviews your claim. Adding a listing and approving its managers are separate steps. You can follow your claim in My Places; no payment is taken during review.

  4. Make it your own

    After ownership approval, your billing contact can subscribe when Bloom is available. All approved managers can then edit the place page, while the billing contact manages the subscription in My Places.

A few things you might be wondering.

Can I subscribe to Thrive today?

Not yet. Thrive is coming soon and is not yet available for purchase. You can email us about your needs without starting a subscription or committing to a purchase.

How much does Thrive cost?

Thrive is $49 USD per month per place. We’ll confirm the included features and terms before subscriptions open.

Will customers buy products on Little Herb Places?

The current plan is a showcase of products or services, not a customer checkout or booking system. Any purchasing or booking experience would need to be defined separately.

Does Thrive include promotion?

Promotion details are still being defined. We are not promising featured placement, newsletter coverage, reach, or sales as part of Thrive.

Should I wait for Thrive to claim my place?

You can request a listing or submit a free ownership claim now. Bloom will be the option for editing your approved listing when purchases open; you do not need to wait for Thrive.
