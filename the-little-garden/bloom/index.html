From finding your place to making it yours.

  1. Find your place

    Search the directory. If your place is missing, send a free listing request and follow its review status in Your Listing Requests.

  2. Claim your listing

    Once the listing is available, open it and choose Claim This Place. Sign in or create a free account, confirm your email, and share your connection to the business.

  3. Get approved

    Our team reviews your claim. Adding a listing and approving its managers are separate steps. You can follow your claim in My Places; no payment is taken during review.

  4. Make it your own

    After ownership approval, your billing contact can choose Bloom when checkout is enabled. All approved managers can then edit the place page, while the billing contact manages the subscription in My Places.

A few things you might be wondering.

Does my place need Bloom to be listed?

No. Requesting a listing and claiming ownership are free. Bloom enables richer listing edits and photo uploads. Essential information corrections remain free; contact our team or use Essential Details when those tools are enabled.

What if my place isn’t in the directory yet?

Send a free listing request first. After our team adds or links the listing, open it and submit an ownership claim. Listing approval does not automatically approve you as a manager.

When do I pay?

Only after your ownership claim has been approved and you choose to subscribe. The site shows whether checkout is currently enabled. There is no charge to send a listing request or ownership claim.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel future renewals in Manage Billing with Stripe from My Places. Editing continues through the paid period, and your published listing stays visible after editing access ends.

Is the price per manager or per place?

Bloom is $19 USD per month per place, plus applicable tax. All approved managers of that place are included; a separate place needs its own subscription.
