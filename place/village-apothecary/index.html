A Little About Village Apothecary

Village Apothecary is a small herbal and supplement shop in Cedar Crest’s East Mountains community. Founded in 2000, the shop carries local Lotus Tree Herbal blends and other botanical products. Its Highway N14 storefront offers a place to browse herbal goods and ask about the selection; call the shop for current visiting hours.
