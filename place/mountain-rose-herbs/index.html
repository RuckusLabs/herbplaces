A Little About Mountain Rose Herbs

Mountain Rose Herbs Mercantile occupies the Farmers’ Union Marketplace in downtown Eugene. The retail shop brings together organic herbs and spices, teas, essential oils, extracts, natural body care, books, and supplies for home herbal projects. Visitors can explore a curated portion of the company’s catalog in a bright marketplace setting, with store-specific events and offerings throughout the year.
