A Little About Sacred Vibes Apothecary

Founded by herbalist Karen Rose, Sacred Vibes Apothecary brings herbal teas, tinctures, and botanical preparations together in Brooklyn. The shop is also home to an educational community centered on spiritual herbalism. Its Art and Practice of Spiritual Herbalism apprenticeship offers a structured path for deeper study.
