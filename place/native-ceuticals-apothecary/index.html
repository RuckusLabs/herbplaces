A Little About Native Ceuticals Apothecary

Native Ceuticals Apothecary is a family-run shop on West Broad Street in downtown Statesville. Its range includes loose teas, herbal extracts and tinctures, mushroom products, topicals, soaps, supplements, and hemp-based goods. The business also hosts community events and invites visitors to browse its varied botanical and personal care selection.
