A Little About Dope Botanicals

Founded by sisters Nakia and Taahirah Stith, Dope Botanicals is a Philadelphia herbal apothecary and bar near Rittenhouse Square. The shop offers herbal teas, tinctures, powders, tonics, and elixirs in a contemporary setting. Its program also includes interactive workshops, classes, consultations, and group coaching, with current opportunities listed on its website.
