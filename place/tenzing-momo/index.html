A Little About Tenzing Momo

Tenzing Momo is an herbal shop in Pike Place Market’s Economy Atrium. Its shelves hold hundreds of herbs and a broad selection of bulk teas, fragrances, essential oils, incense, resins, tinctures, soaps, salves, and bath goods. Tibetan and Buddhist statuary and divination supplies round out the shop, and staff can prepare custom herbal blends.
