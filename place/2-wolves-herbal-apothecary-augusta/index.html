A Little About 2 Wolves Herbal Apothecary - Augusta

2 Wolves Herbal Apothecary’s Augusta location offers herbal tinctures, teas, and other botanical products. The business serves retail and wholesale customers and emphasizes approachable in-store education. Visitors can ask general product questions while browsing. Its current website publishes weekday hours and notes that in-depth consultations are no longer offered.
