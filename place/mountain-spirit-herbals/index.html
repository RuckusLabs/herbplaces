A Little About Mountain Spirit Herbals

Mountain Spirit Herbals is Denise Joy’s small herbal company, established in 1985 in Port Townsend. The catalog spans loose teas, individual tinctures, bulk herbs, essential oils, infused body oils, balms, and creams. Local shopping is available by appointment at the Third Street address; call or text ahead to arrange a visit to this working herbal business.
