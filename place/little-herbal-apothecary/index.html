A Little About Little Herbal Apothecary

Little Herbal Apothecary brings bulk herbs, tinctures, herbal teas, and handmade botanical skincare together in Old Town Lafayette, near Boulder. Founded by clinical herbalist Faith Rodgers, the shop makes its own products in small batches and stocks ingredients for home projects. Workshops, private gatherings, and herbal education invite visitors to learn through making.
