A Little About Terra Apothecary

Terra Apothecary is an owner-operated shop on Denver’s South Broadway with an extensive herb library, essential oils, botanical skincare, and hand-poured candles. Its shelves also showcase artisanal foods and products from local artists and makers. Visit for herbal ingredients, a small gift, or a closer look at the independent businesses represented in the shop.
