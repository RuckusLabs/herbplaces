A Little About The Herb Shop

The Herb Shop offers bulk herbs, teas, spices, herbal extracts, essential oils, and supplements in downtown Grants Pass. Its selection includes botanicals sourced from growers in the Rogue Valley, with organic and wildcrafted options. Browse the storefront for blending ingredients, aromatherapy products, and other herbal supplies.
