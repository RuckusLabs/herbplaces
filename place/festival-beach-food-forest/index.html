A Little About Festival Beach Food Forest

Discover a hidden gem in downtown Austin: the Festival Beach Food Forest! This enchanting edible garden invites you to forage fresh fruits, nuts, herbs, and even medicinal plants right on the shores of Lady Bird Lake.

Here, they harmonize with nature, creating a thriving ecosystem where food, shade, and community flourish. Experience the magic of regenerative practices and let the beauty of this fence-less oasis rejuvenate your spirit.
