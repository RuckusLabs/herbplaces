A Little About Mrs. Thompsons Herbs, Gifts & Folklore

Mrs. Thompson’s Herbs, Gifts & Folklore is the Eugene apothecary associated with clinical herbalist Heather Nic an Fhleisdeir and the Academy of Scottish Herbalism. Its work connects herbalism with aromatherapy, ethnobotany, and plant traditions. The business now welcomes visitors on West Park Street in downtown Eugene, with apothecary hours Tuesday through Saturday afternoons.
