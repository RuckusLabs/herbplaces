A Little About Vibrance Ayurveda

Vibrance Ayurveda is a women-owned Portland practice with an Ayurvedic supply shop and full-service apothecary. Its products include organic bulk herbs and spices, herbal formulas, massage oils, essential oils, locally made ghee, and everyday Ayurvedic accessories. Consultations, education, and traditional body therapies are available by appointment at its Southeast 13th Avenue location.
