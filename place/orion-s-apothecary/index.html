A Little About Orion’s Apothecary

Established in 2019, Orion’s Apothecary offers herbal blends, teas, mushroom products, and locally made botanical goods in Loveland. Its calendar includes tincture-making, tea classes for children, mushroom-growing workshops, and community meetups. Visit the shop to browse ingredients and seasonal products or check the website for upcoming classes.
