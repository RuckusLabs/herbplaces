A Little About Earth Sweet Boutique

Formerly Earth Sweet Botanicals, Earth Sweet Boutique is a downtown Golden shop with herbs, teas, essential oils, herbal tinctures, and botanical bath and body products. Its broader boutique selection includes clothing, accessories, and home goods, with an emphasis on women-owned and farm-to-shelf makers. Classes and events offer opportunities to explore botanicals and make things together.
