A Little About Hoa My Chinese Medicine

Hoa My is a Chinese apothecary in Chicago’s Uptown neighborhood. Its herbal selection includes traditional Chinese herbs and ginseng.

The shop prepares herbal formulas for individual clients and also lists acupuncture and tui na services.
