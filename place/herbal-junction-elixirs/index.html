A Little About Herbal Junction Elixirs

Herbal Junction is a South Eugene maker and elixir bar offering handcrafted herbal drinks and botanical preparations. Its range includes herbal enzyme elixirs, power tonics, tinctures, chai, and blended herbal teas, with recipes drawing on plants from several traditions. Visit the East 25th Avenue location during its published weekday hours to explore its distinctive bottled blends.
