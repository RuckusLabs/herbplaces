A Little About SugarPill Apothecary

SugarPill is a neighborhood apothecary on First Street in Langley, on Whidbey Island. The independent shop combines herbal and personal-care goods with thoughtfully chosen gifts, pantry items, greeting cards, and alcohol-removed wines. Formerly located on Seattle’s Capitol Hill, it now welcomes visitors in Langley and posts changing hours and shop updates through its social channels.
