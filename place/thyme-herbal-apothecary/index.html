A Little About Thyme Herbal Apothecary

Thyme Herbal Apothecary stocks more than 200 organic and sustainably wildcrafted herbs, teas, and spices in Worcester. The shop makes its own herbal tisanes, tinctures, syrups, balms, and oils, alongside a selection of bath products, beeswax candles, and local honey. It has served the neighborhood since 2022.
