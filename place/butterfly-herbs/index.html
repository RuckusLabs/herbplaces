A Little About Butterfly Herbs

Butterfly Herbs is a longstanding downtown Missoula shop combining bulk herbs, teas, spices, and gifts with a coffee bar at the back. Visitors can browse botanical ingredients, scented oils, toiletries, and locally made or imported goods before stopping for a drink. The North Higgins Avenue storefront remains listed by the local visitor bureau; call for current hours.
