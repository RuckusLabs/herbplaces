A Little About Radiance & Kindred Moon Apothecary

Radiance and Kindred Moon Apothecary share a new downtown Olympia location on Fifth Avenue. Their combined shop offers more than 300 varieties of bulk and packaged herbs, teas, and spices alongside tinctures, body care, candles, books, crystals, and gifts. Radiance dates to 1975, with practitioner services available by appointment in addition to daily retail shopping.
