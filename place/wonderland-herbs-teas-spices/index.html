A Little About Wonderland Herbs, Teas & Spices

Wonderland Herbs, Teas & Spices is a family-run shop on Railroad Avenue in downtown Bellingham. Founded by Linda Quintana in 1976, it has grown into a multigenerational business. Its focus remains freshly sourced herbs, teas, and spices, with a long connection to the local community.
