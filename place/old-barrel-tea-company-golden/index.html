A Little About Old Barrel Tea Company — Golden

Old Barrel Tea Company’s Golden shop sits in the historic Foss Building on Washington Avenue. Browse loose-leaf teas and herbal blends, local honey, spices, and brewing accessories in a cozy downtown setting. The Golden location is part of a small, family-founded regional tea business with shops across the Southwest.
