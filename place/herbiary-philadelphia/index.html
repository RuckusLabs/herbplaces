A Little About Herbiary Philadelphia

Herbiary’s Philadelphia location sits inside Reading Terminal Market, offering bulk teas, essential oils, tinctures, soaps, incense, and botanical body care. Shoppers can also browse books on herbs, foraging, and gardening. This market stall is one of the business’s two locations and publishes daily shopping hours through the market and its own website.
