A Little About Dragon Herbarium

Dragon Herbarium carries more than 400 bulk organic herbs and teas, along with botanical supplies and gifts. The Keith family has operated the business for over 40 years, building on a shop first opened in the 1970s. Visit its Beaverton-Hillsdale Highway storefront for ingredients and blends.
