A Little About Herbiary Asheville

Herbiary’s Asheville shop brings together herbs, loose teas, essential oils, tinctures, botanical body care, books, and supplies for home preparations. Located on North Market Street near downtown, the shop is part of a two-location business now under new ownership. Its botanical selection includes products from local growers and makers.
