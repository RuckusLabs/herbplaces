A Little About Casablanca Herbal Shop

Casablanca Herbal Shop is a neighborhood source for herbal goods on Fayetteville Street in Durham. Visitors can browse its botanical selection and ask the staff about available products. Whipped Cream Body identifies the shop as a local pickup location for its Chebe scalp oil, connecting the store with a local botanical body care maker.
