A Little About Chandra Botanicals

Chandra Botanicals connects herbal education, plant-based food, and handmade botanical cosmetics. Its services include herbal workshops, individual consultations, private shopping appointments, and chef services. Workshop topics range from infused oils and lotion making to seasonal foods. The town’s shopping directory locates the business on Atlantic Beach Causeway; contact ahead to arrange a visit.
