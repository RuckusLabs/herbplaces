A Little About Smile Herb Shop

Smile Herb Shop is a long-running College Park herb store housed in a historic home on Berwyn Road. The shop stocks bulk herbs and spices, teas, essential oils, tinctures, supplements, and body care products. Visitors can also browse books, gifts, and supplies, with classes and a garden adding to the neighborhood setting.
