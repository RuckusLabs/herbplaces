A Little About Little House of Healing Herbs Apothecary

Little House of Healing Herbs Apothecary is an independent merchant inside Zen & Alchemy in downtown Kerrville. Its display includes jars of loose herbal blends, packaged botanical goods, sprays, and bottled preparations. Visitors can browse the apothecary alongside the host shop’s other merchants offering handmade items, gifts, artwork, and wellness products.
