A Little About Merz Apothecary

Merz Apothecary is a family-owned Chicago shop established in 1875. Its selection spans herbal products, teas, supplements and personal care items.

The Lincoln Square location offers in-store shopping and curbside pickup. Its long-running shop is located on North Lincoln Avenue.
