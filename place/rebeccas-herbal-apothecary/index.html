A Little About Rebecca’s Herbal Apothecary & Supply

Rebecca’s Herbal Apothecary & Supply has welcomed Boulder’s plant community since 2004. A wall of dried herbs anchors the shop, alongside books, bulk ingredients, containers, and handcrafted botanical products made on site. Herbal classes and its Rocky Mountain Community Herbalism School offer ways to learn more about plants, sourcing, and making your own preparations.
