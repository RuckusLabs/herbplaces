A Little About Red Clover Herbs and Market

Red Clover Herbs & Market is a family-run Idaho Falls shop carrying nearly 200 bulk dried herbs and spices. Its shelves include the house Harmony Blend herbal tea, essential oils, herbal supplements, local honey, goat-milk soaps, books, and tea-making accessories. Shoppers can browse the Woodruff Avenue store during weekday hours or explore selected products online.
