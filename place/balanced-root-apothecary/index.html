A Little About Balanced Root Apothecary

An independent, woman-owned apothecary in Denver’s Capitol Hill neighborhood, Balanced Root carries bulk herbs, tinctures, herbal teas, and body care. Browse its shelves for single herbs or ask about a custom tea or tincture blend. The shop also offers classes and herbalist guidance, bringing botanical ingredients and practical plant knowledge into a welcoming neighborhood space.
