A Little About Herban Wellness

Herban Wellness carries bulk herbs and powders, house-blended teas and tinctures, essential oils, and botanical skincare in downtown Kirkland. Custom blends, herbal consultations, and educational events complement the retail selection. Visit the shop to explore ingredients or ask about arranging a consultation.
