A Little About Meadowsweet

Meadowsweet Herbs is a woman-owned, family business staffed by trained herbalists on Missoula’s Hip Strip. The shop carries bulk herbs and tinctures, an essential oil blending bar, tea, botanical body care, books, gifts, and supplies for making herbal preparations. Its own salves and infused products sit alongside local makers and selected national brands.
