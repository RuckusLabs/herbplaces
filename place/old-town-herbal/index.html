A Little About Old Town Herbal

Old Town Herbal offers dried herbs, loose-leaf teas, tinctures, botanical body care, and locally made goods in Albuquerque’s Old Town.

The shop also publishes a calendar of herbal classes and seasonal workshops. Check directly for current hours and individual event details.
