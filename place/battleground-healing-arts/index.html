A Little About Battleground Healing Arts

Healing Arts Apothecary is part of Battle Ground Healing Arts on East Main Street. Its offerings include dried herbs, herbal tinctures, custom tea blends, mushrooms, skin care, candles, and locally made art. The center also hosts classes and individual practitioner appointments, giving visitors several ways to explore herbal traditions and botanical products.
