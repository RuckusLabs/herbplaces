A Little About Simply Herbal Organics

Simply Herbal Organics combines a Peachland retail shop with an online apothecary. Its shelves include handcrafted herbal blends, essential oils, personal care products, and selected local or specialty foods. The business also offers custom preparations, individual herbal consultations, and classes or demonstrations for groups interested in making and using herbal preparations.
