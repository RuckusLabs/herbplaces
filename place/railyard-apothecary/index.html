A Little About Railyard Apothecary

Railyard Apothecary is a worker-owned herbal co-op in Burlington’s South End. Its shop sources herbs from local farms and forests as well as organic distributors, and offers bulk botanicals, preparations, classes, and private herbal consultations. The Howard Street space welcomes in-person shoppers, with Monday masking hours designed to support greater access.
