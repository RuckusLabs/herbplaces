A Little About Musa Moon

Musa Moon is a bulk herb shop on West Morehead Street in Charlotte. Its selection includes loose herbs, custom tea blends, tinctures, essential oils, sea moss products, and tea accessories. Visitors can browse the shop’s botanical offerings or book a consultation, with current shopping hours published on its website.
