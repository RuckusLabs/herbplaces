A Little About Erin’s Meadow Herb Farm

Erin’s Meadow Herb Farm is a twelve-acre destination near Clinton with herb gardens, greenhouses, a shop, and a kitchen classroom. Visitors can find herb plants, bulk dried botanicals, tea blends, essential oils, books, and preparation supplies. Seasonal classes cover growing herbs, cooking, crafts, and herbal preparations; opening days vary throughout the year.
