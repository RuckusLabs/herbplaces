A Little About Hearth and Seoul Herbal Shop

Hearth and Seoul is a neighborhood apothecary in Denver’s Highlands area with bulk herbs, Korean teas, custom blends, and botanical goods. Its classes explore tea blending, herbalism, and cultural traditions, with community herbal support offered by appointment. Stop in to browse herbs by the ounce and discover teas sourced from suppliers in Colorado and South Korea.
