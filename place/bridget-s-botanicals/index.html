A Little About Bridget’s Botanicals

Bridget’s Botanicals combines a downtown Littleton apothecary with botanical education and private events. Founded by a scientist and certified herbalist, the business makes small-batch products with sustainably sourced ingredients. Its workshops include tea blending, aromatherapy mists, and botanical crafts, with a focus on women’s wellness.
