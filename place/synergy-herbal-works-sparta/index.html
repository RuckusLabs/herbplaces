A Little About Synergy Herbal Works - Sparta

Synergy Herbal Works’ Sparta shop offers a broad selection of organic and wildcrafted botanicals, loose-leaf teas, tinctures, spices, and handmade body care. Founded by registered herbalist Sherri Stickler, the business combines Tennessee-made products with herbal classes and educational resources in a downtown storefront on Liberty Square.
