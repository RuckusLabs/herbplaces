A Little About Tendril Herbal Apothecary and Healing Center

Tendril Herbal Apothecary and Healing Center brings an herb shop and practitioner space together on Sandpoint’s Main Street. Its herbalists prepare teas, tinctures, syrups, and other botanical blends, with consultations and educational offerings available. The center also hosts a range of separately scheduled services, and visitors can browse the apothecary during its published weekday hours.
