A Little About Wild Soul River

Wild Soul River is a small herbal shop and gathering space on Cole Avenue in Williamstown. Visitors can explore bulk herbs, house-made tea blends and tinctures, books, crystals, and other botanical goods, or stop for tea and coffee. On-site herbalists also offer individual consultations, workshops, and other appointments.
