A Little About Living Earth Herbs

Living Earth Herbs is a downtown Bellingham apothecary with organic bulk herbs, teas, tinctures, essential oils, salves, and flower essences. The shop makes custom tea, tincture, and essential oil blends and carries locally made botanical goods. Its Cornwall Avenue location is close to the downtown transit hub, making it an easy stop for neighborhood shoppers.
