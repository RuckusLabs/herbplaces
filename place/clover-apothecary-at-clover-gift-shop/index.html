A Little About Clover Apothecary at Clover Gift Shop

Clover Apothecary is the house botanical product line at Clover Gift Shop, a Woodstock business established in 1928. The shop offers locally crafted salves, lotions, skin creams, and hemp-derived CBD products alongside gifts and home goods. It supports Vermont makers and hosts workshops and product demonstrations.
