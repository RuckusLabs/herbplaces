A Little About Soulful Body & Mind Apothecary

Soulful Body & Mind Apothecary is a plant and lifestyle gift shop on Elm Street in Baldwinville. The shop carries bulk herbs and flowers, teas, bath and body products, and goods from local makers. Its selection also includes plants, crystals, art, and gifts. The shop participates in the 2026 Country Roads holiday shopping tour.
